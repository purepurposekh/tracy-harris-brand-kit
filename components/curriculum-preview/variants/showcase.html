What's inside.

The Freedom Filled® curriculum.

Eight modules released over twelve months. Each one lands with a workbook, a live coaching call, and a Voxer thread for questions as you go.

    01

    The Foundation.

    Your vision, your values, your version of Freedom Filled® success.
    We start where every real business starts: with you. By the end of Module 1 you'll have your definition of success mapped across F.R.E.S.H., a one-page vision document, and a non-negotiable week template.
    • F.R.E.S.H. self-assessment + gap analysis
    • One-page vision exercise (your 12-month snapshot)
    • Non-negotiable week template + energy audit
    02

    Your Signature Offer.

    The one offer you'll get known for. Priced, packaged, positioned.
    Stop spinning offers. Build the one. You'll design your signature offer using the Freedom Filled® Offers™ framework, price it properly, and walk away with delivery architecture that doesn't drain you.
    03

    The Million Dollar Message.

    Messaging clarity that makes right-fit women say "this is for me".
    Your Million Dollar Message™, built in a week. Story bank, signature phrases, and the one-sentence pitch you'll use in every intro DM, sales call, and podcast interview from here on.
    04

    The Content Engine.

    A repeatable system for content that sells without burning you out.
    Batch-ready content system that pumps out one anchor piece per week and repurposes it across Instagram, email, and podcast. Built for four-day weeks.
    05

    Sales in your voice.

    Selling that feels warm, not slimy. Apply-based not pressure-based.
    Sales conversations, application forms, and objection-handling scripts. No fake scarcity, no gross countdowns. Just clear invitations to right-fit women.
    06

    Launches that don't break you.

    Peaceful-scale launch plans built around a 300/100/10 workshop.
    My exact workshop model: 300 registrations, 100 attend, 10 enrol. Tear-down of what makes it convert, plus your fill-in-the-blanks launch calendar.
    07

    Systems + Support.

    The tech stack and team structure that holds a scaling business.
    What to build yourself, what to hand off, and when. Including the exact Asana setup, Loom SOP template, and first-hire scorecards.
    08

    The Peaceful Scale.

    Growing revenue without growing your hours or your stress.
    The mindset and metrics that turn a six-figure year into a seven-figure rhythm. Grounded, sustainable, stewarded.
