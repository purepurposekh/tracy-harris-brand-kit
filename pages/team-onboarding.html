01 · Vision

Where we are going.

Women leading thriving, freedom-filled businesses by sharing their wisdom, teaching transformational work, and building communities that lift each other higher.

A world where more women say yes to the calling on their life, and have the strategy, support, and spaciousness to actually build it.

02 · Mission

What we do.

We help women build freedom-filled businesses. Not hustle-harder businesses. Not grind-till-you-break businesses. Real, beautiful, profitable businesses that serve the life they want to live.

We do that through mentorship (FFB), mastermind (FFM), and a body of work that teaches women to scale with strategy, stewardship, and soul.

03 · Values

What we stand on.

These eight values are the filter. When a decision is hard, when a message is unclear, when the way forward is murky, you run it through these.

  1. 01

    Integrity

    We do what we said we would do. We tell the truth, even when it costs us. What happens in private matches what happens in public.

  2. 02

    Leading with love

    Every message, every decision, every difficult conversation starts from a posture of love. Firm when it needs to be. Always kind.

  3. 03

    Collaboration

    We build with, not at. Team, clients, partners. The best ideas win, not the loudest voice. We share credit generously.

  4. 04

    Striving for excellence

    Good enough is rarely good enough. We sweat the details clients notice and the ones they do not. Excellence is a habit, not a performance.

  5. 05

    Radical responsibility

    If it is ours, we own it fully. If it broke, we fix it. If we missed it, we name it. No blame, no spin, no waiting to be rescued.

  6. 06

    Initiative

    See the gap, close the gap. We do not wait to be told. We bring the RISE brief, the suggested fix, the next step. Then we move.

  7. 07

    Communication

    Clear beats clever. We over-communicate context, under-communicate noise. If it affects someone else, they hear it from us first.

  8. 08

    Unparalleled experience

    From a cold DM to a retreat in Bali, every touchpoint feels considered, warm, and alive. The bar is, would Tracy be proud to send this?

05 · Operational framework

RISE, how we communicate and decide.

RISE is how we bring problems, decisions, and proposals to the team. It replaces rambling Slack messages and circular back-and-forth with a structure that respects everyone's time, especially Tracy's.

Recognise · Investigate · Suggest · Execute.

  1. R

    Recognise

    State the situation, problem, or opportunity. Just the facts. What is happening, what triggered this. Do not jump to the fix yet.

  2. I

    Investigate

    Gather context. What do we know, what have we tried, what are the constraints, what is the relevant history. Show your thinking.

  3. S

    Suggest

    Bring a recommendation. One path forward, or options if there is more than one viable route. Be specific. Show the trade-offs.

  4. E

    Execute

    Define next steps, owner, and timeline. Once a decision is made, move. Do not loop back into investigation.

RISE respects Tracy's limited working hours. When the team uses it, Tracy can read a brief and make a decision in minutes, not hours.
