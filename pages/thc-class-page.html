In case you're wondering

Frequently asked questions.

Do I need to attend the workshop live?

You don't. The workshop is best live because the Q&A is what makes it sing, but a recording will be sent to everyone who registers. If you can't make it, register anyway and you'll get the replay.

What's the difference between the workshop and the quiz?

The class you just watched is the overview. The workshop is the depth. Ninety minutes with me live, going deeper into the pathway, with case studies and Q&A.

The quiz is the personalisation. Five minutes, async. You'll know your loudest symptom, your starting point, and whether Freedom Filled® Business is the right container for you specifically.

How do I know if Freedom Filled® Business is for me?

The quiz tells you, honestly. It's a five-minute diagnostic that maps your situation to whether FFB fits. If it's the right next step, the quiz says so. If it isn't yet, the quiz points you to what is. We'd rather you take the path that actually fits than the one we're selling.

How long do I have access to the class recording?

As long as you want. You can re-watch any time at this URL, and the Pathway Map PDF lives on your computer once you download it. The class isn't going anywhere.

What if I'm just starting out?

The class still works for you. The pathway is the same whether you're at month two or month two hundred. What changes is which symptom is loudest, and where your starting point sits on the Build / Sell / Lead map. Take the quiz to find out.

Will Tracy actually read what I write if I reply?

Yes. Replies to her emails go straight to her inbox. She reads them, even when she doesn't reply to every one personally. If you have a question or a story, send it.
